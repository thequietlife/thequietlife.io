🦙 setting up local AI

November 2026

I wanted to experiment with running AI models locally, so I put together a small setup and took notes along the way.

Why run a model locally?

Running a model on your own machine can be useful for learning, experimenting, and working without relying on a hosted interface. The experience depends on your hardware and the model you choose.

The setup

I started by checking the machine’s available memory and storage, then chose a model that fit those limits. From there, I installed the runtime, downloaded the model, and tested it with a few prompts.

Notes

The main lesson: start small, keep track of what you install, and test one change at a time. I’ve collected the commands and setup details in the project repository.
