setting up local AI

My goal is to install and run Ollama locally. Why? It doesn't need WI-FI or data and is allegedly private, nothing will leave my house. My hardware is a woeful 8GB Lenovo ThinkCentre m910q but I want to use what I have right now. From what I can see you can use ollama even with 8GB by selecting a small model. I tried qwen2.5:3b. It works without WI-FI. But some of the info it was serving up was just wrong. I asked a couple of random questions like who was Molly Meldrum and Kylie Minogue. Both responses were wrong. I tried to have it analyse a pdf document, Sherman Kent and the Profession of Intelligence Analysis, but it didn't really provide the key parts of the doc. It just gave me a timeline of his life and list of published works. I was after 'Kent’s Analytic Doctrine'. I've ordered two 16GB RAM sticks which will push up the m910q to 32GB and let me use bigger AI models.

hardware

* 8GB Lenovo ThinkCentre m910q * waiting on 2 x 16GB RAM sticks (G.Skill Ripjaws 16GB 3200MHz DDR4 SODIMM.)
