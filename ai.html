My goal is to install and run Ollama locally. Why? It can run without Wi-Fi or a data connection, and is allegedly private—nothing will leave my house.

My hardware is a woeful 8GB Lenovo ThinkCentre M910q, but I want to use what I have right now. From what I can see, Ollama can run with 8GB by selecting a small model.

I tried qwen2.5:3b. It works without Wi-Fi, but some of the information it gave me was wrong. I asked a couple of random questions, like who Molly Meldrum and Kylie Minogue were. Both responses were wrong.

I tried to get it to analyse a PDF document, Sherman Kent and the Profession of Intelligence Analysis , but it didn’t identify the key parts of the document. It gave me a timeline of his life and a list of published works. I was looking for “Kent’s Analytic Doctrine.”

I’ve ordered two 16GB RAM sticks, which will bring the M910q up to 32GB and let me try larger AI models.

Hardware

  • 8GB Lenovo ThinkCentre M910q
  • Waiting on 2 × 16GB RAM sticks (G.Skill Ripjaws 16GB 3200MHz DDR4 SODIMM)
